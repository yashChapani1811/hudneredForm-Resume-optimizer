Cover Letter Templates That Actually Work

A good cover letter is not a repeat of your resume. It connects your best proof to the employer's specific problem.

February 28, 20245 min readHunderedform Team

Use a simple structure

Open with the role and why you are interested. Then show two or three pieces of evidence that match the job.

Make it specific to the company

One sentence of real context is better than a paragraph of generic excitement. Mention a product, team, market, or challenge that connects to your skills.

Most cover letters should fit on one page with direct paragraphs. Hiring teams need clarity more than formality.

End with confidence

Close by pointing back to the value you can bring and your interest in speaking further.
