Your Data

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Hunderedform collects, how we use it, and the controls you have when you use our free ATS resume checker and AI resume optimizer.

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Policy Summary

At a Glance

  • We use resume data to provide resume analysis and optimization feedback.
  • We do not sell your personal information or resume content.
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data.

Sections

Information We Collect How We Use Information AI Processing and Resume Uploads How We Share Information Data Retention Security Your Privacy Rights Children Contact
1

Information We Collect

Account and contact information, such as your name, email address, support messages, and plan details.

Resume and job search content you choose to upload, paste, or submit for analysis, including resume text, job descriptions, and related career information.

Technical and usage data, including IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, referring URLs, approximate location, and interaction events.

Payment details are processed by payment providers. Hunderedform does not store full card numbers on its own servers.

2

How We Use Information

We use your information to provide ATS scoring, AI resume feedback, keyword matching, account features, support, security, analytics, and service improvements.

We may use aggregated or de-identified information to understand product performance and improve resume analysis quality.

We use email only for account notices, support replies, product updates, and marketing messages where permitted by law. You can unsubscribe from non-essential marketing.

3

AI Processing and Resume Uploads

When you request resume analysis, resume content may be sent to AI infrastructure or model providers solely to generate your requested feedback.

Do not upload sensitive information that is not needed for a resume review, such as government identity numbers, bank details, medical records, or passwords.

AI output is advisory. You remain responsible for reviewing suggestions before using them in an application.

4

How We Share Information

We share information with service providers that help us operate Hunderedform, such as hosting, analytics, security, email delivery, payments, and AI processing.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or Hunderedform, to investigate abuse, or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

We do not sell resume content or personal information to recruiters, advertisers, or data brokers.

5

Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law.

Resume uploads may be retained for account features, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, or user-requested history. You can request deletion at any time.

Analytics records may be retained in aggregated or de-identified form after account data is deleted.

6

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information.

No online service can guarantee perfect security. Please use strong passwords and avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive information.

7

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to certain processing of personal information.

To make a privacy request, contact privacy@hunderedform.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing the request.

8

Children

Hunderedform is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to privacy@hunderedform.com or through the contact page.
