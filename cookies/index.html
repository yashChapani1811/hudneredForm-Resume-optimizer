Cookie Policy

Learn how HundredForm uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience.

Last updated: April 23, 2024

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help us provide better services by remembering your preferences and improving site functionality.

1. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies for the following purposes:

Essential Cookies

Required for basic website functionality:

  • Maintaining user sessions
  • Remembering login status
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Load balancing

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how our site is used:

  • Page views and user behavior
  • Time spent on pages
  • Navigation paths
  • Device and browser information

Preference Cookies

Remember your choices and settings:

  • Language preferences
  • Theme and display settings
  • Remembered form data
  • Customization options

Marketing Cookies

Used for advertising and marketing:

  • Personalized advertisements
  • Campaign performance tracking
  • Retargeting and remarketing
  • Cross-site behavioral tracking

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Session Cookies

Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser. Used for:

  • Maintaining your session during website visits
  • Tracking your progress through multi-step processes
  • Temporary storage of form data

Persistent Cookies

Cookies that remain on your device for a set period. Used for:

  • Remembering your login preferences
  • Tracking long-term user behavior patterns
  • Personalization across sessions

Third-Party Cookies

Cookies set by external services we use. These include:

  • Google Analytics for website analytics
  • Payment processors for transaction security
  • Social media platforms for sharing features
  • Advertising networks for targeted ads

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Accept/Reject: Choose which types of cookies to accept
  • Delete: Remove existing cookies from your device
  • Block: Prevent certain websites from setting cookies
  • Notifications: Receive alerts when cookies are set

Important Note

Blocking essential cookies may affect website functionality and prevent you from using certain features of our service.

Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner where you can:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies
  • Customize your cookie preferences
  • Change preferences at any time

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may set cookies:

Google Analytics

Used for website analytics and user behavior tracking. Google Analytics sets cookies to:

  • Distinguish unique users
  • Track session duration
  • Monitor page interactions

Google Privacy Policy

Payment Processors

Secure payment processing services (Stripe, PayPal) set cookies for:

  • Payment security and fraud prevention
  • Remembering payment preferences
  • Transaction tracking

Social Media Platforms

Social sharing features may set cookies for:

  • Social media authentication
  • Content sharing functionality
  • Social media analytics

6. Your Rights Regarding Cookies

You have the following rights regarding cookies:

  • Right to Information: Know what cookies are being used and why
  • Right to Consent: Choose which cookies to accept
  • Right to Withdraw: Change your cookie preferences at any time
  • Right to Object: Opt out of certain types of cookies

You can exercise these rights through our cookie consent banner or your browser settings.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this cookie policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws.

We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy on our website
  • Updating the "Last updated" date
  • Displaying a notice on our platform
  • Sending email notifications for significant changes

Your continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
